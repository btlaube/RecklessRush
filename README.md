# Reckless Rush
You're late. Again.

**Reckless Rush** is a driving game about running out of time. Every run is a point-to-point race against a clock you can't beat. When you cross the finish, the game tells you exactly how late you were, and what it cost the city along the way.

---

## How it plays

- **You're always late.** Each level gives you an objective and a deadline. The clock starts when the countdown ends, and it never runs your way.
-- **Note:** Currently only one level, with more planned for the future.
- **Break things.** Fire hydrants, lamp posts, cones, signs, fences and more can be knocked loose, toppled or destroyed. Every object has a price, and the damage you cause adds up. Your own car takes damage too, scaled by how hard you hit.
- **Check your results.** At the finish you see how early or late you were, and the property damage you caused.
- **Different roads.** Weave through busy downtown traffic, try to find the fastest path.

---

## Controls

- **Steer / accelerate / brake:** [WASD or arrow keys]
- **Handbrake:** Space
- **Start a run:** click, then wait for the countdown
